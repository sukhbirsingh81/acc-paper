\begin{abstract}
Urban Air Mobility (UAM) requires scalable path-planning solutions to safely integrate large numbers of eVTOL vehicles into structured low-altitude airspace. U-space, the European framework for unmanned aircraft traffic management in low-altitude airspace, relies on trajectory-based planning services to support such operations. Although routing algorithms, 4D trajectory generation, and deconfliction are well established individually, few studies report architecture-level performance results of a service-oriented implementation. This paper presents a service-oriented 4D path-planning framework for U-space corridor operations that combines A*-based global routing on a corridor graph, CFMS-based time-parameterized trajectory generation, and a separate NDMap-based deconfliction service. The system follows a microservice-oriented architecture separating a topology service providing vertiports and corridor networks, the \texttt{acc-trajcalc} path-planning service performing routing and trajectory generation, the \texttt{acc-trajdeconf} service performing deconfliction, and an interface to a U-space Service Provider (USSP). Computational performance is evaluated on a representative vertiport corridor network using sequential trajectory requests, with runtime explicitly separated into A* routing, waypoint synthesis, CFMS invocation and trajectory generation, and the planner-stage subtotal.
\end{abstract}
